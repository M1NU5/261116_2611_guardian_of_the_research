- A qualitative research method that enables you to derive new theories based on the iterative collection and analysis of real world data.
![[Pasted image 20261027145359.png]]
- Use Grounded Theory when there is no existing theory that explains a phenomenon or there is an existing theory but the data is incomplete e.g. data is collected from participants in Germany but no-where else .
- Start with raw data (transcripts etc), turn that into a theory, 'grounded in the data'
- Data collection and analysis is cyclical:
	- Collected data (Theoretical Sampling) from small group of people (more later)
	- Turn transcripts into excerpts and codify them (these codes may change over time). Can use [[Open Coding]]
	- Go back into the field and collect more data
	- Find connections between codes/categories (Axial coding)
	- Go collect more data
	- Refine codes and categories
	- The Core category that connects all the codes together and is the basis for the theory
