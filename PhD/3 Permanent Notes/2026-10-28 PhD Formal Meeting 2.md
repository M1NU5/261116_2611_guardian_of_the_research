- Reviewed [[Literature Note - Locke, E. A., & Latham, G. P. (2002). Building a practically useful theory of goal setting and task motivation - A 35-year odyssey]]
	- Key Point:  Specific and difficult goals promote higher performance and immediate feedback is crucial.
- Reviewed [[Literature Note - Leroy, S. (2009). Why is it so hard to do my work The challenge of attention residue when switching between work tasks]]
	- Key Point: Concept of Attention Residue is a significant problem when context switching. Finishing Task A and under high time pressure, creates confidence in Task A. This confidence improves performance in Task B.
- Reviewed [[Literature Note - Monsell, S. (2003). Task switching - Trends in Cognitive Sciences, 7(3), 134-140.]]
	- Key Point: Introduces the idea of Task-Set-Reconfiguration, which is the time/effort/resources required to 'change gears in the brain'. Performance is better when a longer time has passed before returning to an uncompleted task. Is this due to that specific TSR recovering, so rotating around TSRs throughout the day may be useful when one must context switch?
