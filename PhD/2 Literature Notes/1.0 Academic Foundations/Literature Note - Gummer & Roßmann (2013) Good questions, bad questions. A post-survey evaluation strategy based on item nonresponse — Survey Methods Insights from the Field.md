# Key Point
If you don't ask questions people can and want to answer, then they don't respond.
  
# Notes  
- This peer-reviewed methodological article discusses how item nonresponse (people skipping questions) can be used to identify flawed or poorly designed questions in a survey. It explicitly links high item nonresponse to problematic question wording or construction — meaning if surveys don’t ask questions in a way respondents can or want to answer, they simply don’t respond.
