## Understanding Three Types of Sources
- Primary - For social sciences these are
	- Census
	- Survey Data
	- Interviews
	- Fieldwork (ethnographic observation)
	- Experimentation 
- Secondary - Reports etc based on primary sources that are peer reviewed. Available at EBSCO Host and Google Scholar etc 
- Tertiary - Material for the General reader such as books, magazines (Psychology Today) encyclopaedias (including Wikipedia), YouTube etc. They can give an overview of secondary sources and intros to new material/ideas.
